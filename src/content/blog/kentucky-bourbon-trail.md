The boys made it to Kentucky. The bourbon trail was not ready for us.

Right upon landing I was told to meet at the bar by Corey. I was given a bourbon and beer pairing.
Little did I know this would be the start of 72 hours of drinking.

After Matt joined us we decided to go to Angel's Envy in Louisville. Sky told us he was only an hour
away, but his location did not tell us the same story. It would be two hours. So we went to Angel's,
the place I was most excited for.

## Angel's Envy

Angel's did not disappoint and went above and beyond. We did the archive flight, full of bourbon not
available for retail. All were delicious, some were tastier than the others. We also got an old
fashioned.

> The Angel's Envy old fashioned would be the best old fashioned while in bourbon.

Only three of us were there to score it, and it still finished first overall. Sky, Zach and Bryce
missed this one, hence the N/A's.

## The standings

### 1. Angel's Envy - 8.75

The one I was most excited about, and the one that earned it. Archive flight, an old fashioned worth
writing down, and staff who kept going after they did not have to.

### 2. Castle & Key - 8.29

The most agreed-upon stop of the trip. Six scores, all between 8 and 9. Nobody loved it more than
anyone else and nobody had a bad word, which almost never happens with this group.

### 3. Woodford - 8.08

Same story as Castle & Key, half a point lower. Six scores, spread of 7.5 to 9. Matt's 9 was the high
mark.

### 4. Jim Beam - 7.75

The most divisive of the good ones. Matt gave it a 9.5, the highest single score anybody gave anything
all weekend. Bryce gave it a 6. Both of them were standing in the same room.

### 5. Wise Bird - 7.33

Cider, not bourbon, and it still beat four distilleries. Only three of us scored it and we landed
within half a point of each other: 7, 7.5, 7.5.

### 6. Rabbit Hole - 6.83

The most boring result on the board. Six scores, and five of them were a 6 or a 7. Zach's 8 was the
only one with any conviction in it.

### 7. Wild Turkey - 6.46

Corey had it at 8, Zach had it at 4. Wide open, and the average lands somewhere neither of them
actually thinks.

### 8. Bardstown - 6.17

The biggest argument of the trip. Matt gave it a 9. Zach gave it a 1. That is an eight point spread on
the same tour, the same pours, the same afternoon. Someone is wrong and it is not going to be settled
here.

### 9. Four Roses - 2.67

The only genuine consensus of the weekend, and it was a consensus that something had gone wrong. Three
of the six scores were a 1 or a 2. Sky's 5 was the kindest thing anyone said. Nothing else all
weekend came within three and a half points of being this bad.
