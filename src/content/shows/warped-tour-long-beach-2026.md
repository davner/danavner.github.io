Warped Tour, Long Beach. Too many bands across too many stages to write an honest
lineup, so this one stays general — a whole day of running between stages, catching
half sets, and finding two or three new favourites by accident.

The best part of a festival is the same as the best part of a small show: something
you did not plan on seeing turns out to be the thing you talk about afterwards.
