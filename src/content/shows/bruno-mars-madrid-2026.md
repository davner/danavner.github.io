Three acts, and he moved us through all of them on purpose.

The first got the party going, straight into the songs everyone came to shout.
Then he brought out Anderson .Paak for Silk Sonic and the whole place changed
gear - the lights dropped low and the vibe was set.

The third act is the one that got us. Classic love songs, and we were crying.
