For years this domain was one page. A photo, a line about what I do, a list of projects. It did the
one job I asked of it, which was to show up when someone searched my name.

I kept it that way because some part of me was managing who might see the rest. The records, the
Fortnite hours, the show I drove too far for, the Lego set still half-built on the shelf. None of it
is impressive, and that was the whole problem, right up until it stopped being one.

So this is the other version. A place to put the things I like, reviews nobody asked for, and
memories I would rather keep somewhere that isn't a dying app. I am done trimming myself down to the
parts that look good on a résumé. If it's cringe, then it's cringe, and it's mine. That turns out to
be the freeing part: once you stop writing for the people who might judge it, there is a lot more you
actually want to say.

That is most of what this site is now. About is the rest of me. Blog is this, whatever has my
attention that week. Shows is every gig I have been to, logged and rated, and somehow the section I
update most.

If you came here for the polished version, it still exists. The day job has a Career page, and the
consulting I actually take on runs through [Sidereal Software](https://sidereal.software): observatory
systems and the software scientists have to trust at 3 a.m. That is the door for work. Everything
else here is for the parts of me that work never sees.

No schedule, no newsletter. It is a place to put things.
