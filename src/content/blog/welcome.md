For a long time this domain was a single page: a photo, a sentence about what I do, and a list of
projects. It worked, in the sense that anyone who searched my name found something. It did not work
in the sense that there was nowhere to put anything else.

So this is the rebuild. Three doors instead of one page.

**Work** is the part you would expect — projects, roles, the stack I actually reach for. If you
landed here from a résumé or a conference badge, that is probably the page you want.

**About** is the rest of me. Where I have lived, what I do when the laptop is closed, and one
oddly specific bowling statistic that I will be bringing up for the rest of my life.

**Writing** is this. Notes, mostly. Some of it will be about building software for astronomy —
that is where most of my thinking goes, and there is a shortage of people writing honestly about
what it is like to ship production systems inside a science organization. Some of it will not be
about that at all. Lifting, drums, astrophotography from a light-polluted backyard, whatever has my
attention.

I went back and forth on whether to split those into two blogs. I decided not to. Every post is
tagged **Work** or **Personal**, and you can filter by either, but they live in the same place
because that is how they actually happen — the same person is doing both, usually in the same week.
Pretending otherwise seemed like more effort than it was worth.

No schedule, no newsletter, no promises about frequency. Just a place to put things.
