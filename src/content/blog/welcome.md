For a long time this domain was one page. A photo, a sentence about what I do, a list of projects.
Anyone who searched my name found it, which was the whole job it had, but there was nowhere to put
anything else.

So I rebuilt it as four sections instead.

About is the rest of me: where I have lived, what I do once the laptop is shut, and one oddly
specific bowling statistic I intend to bring up for the rest of my life. Career is the part you
would expect, so if you got here from a résumé or a conference badge, start there.

Blog is this. Mostly notes. Some of it will be about building software for astronomy, partly because
that is where most of my thinking goes and partly because almost nobody writes honestly about what
it is like to ship production systems inside a science organization. The rest will have nothing to
do with that. Records, lifting, astrophotography from a light-polluted backyard, whatever is in
front of me.

Shows is a log of every gig I have been to. It has turned into the section I update most, which
surprised me.

I went back and forth on splitting the writing into two blogs and decided against it. Posts are
tagged Work or Personal and you can filter by either, but they sit in the same place because that is
how they actually happen: the same person, usually in the same week. Pretending otherwise seemed
like more work than it was worth.

No schedule and no newsletter. It is a place to put things.
