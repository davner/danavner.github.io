Had a hopping good time seeing **Dark Dazey**. 

I actually discovered them from a bumper sticker in Pasadena. The album name was so unique that I had to look them up, and I’ve been listening ever since.

Of course, seeing them play in Frogtown made me even more excited, considering the band describes themselves as **#frogrock**.

The crowd was electric. Their friends and family really came out and kept the energy so high all night. We were seconds away from playing leapfrog in the pit.

I’m excited to see what these dudes get up to in the future. 🐸🤘
